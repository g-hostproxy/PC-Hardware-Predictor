Launch watch: catching MSRP on day one

No new gaming GPU has an announced price yet. Here's what's expected and how to improve your odds when one arrives.

Low chance of MSRP stock

RTX 50 Super

January 2027 at the earliest, and reportedly shelved. MSRP not announced. Memory costs make a low list price unlikely.

Medium chance

AMD's first RDNA 5 chip

A single mid-range chip is rumored for 2027, tied to Xbox Project Helix. MSRP not announced. AMD has held closer to list price than Nvidia.

Unknown

RTX 60 and full RDNA 5

Reportedly 2028. Too far out to plan around, but the first weeks of a new generation are when list price is most reachable.

Unlikely

Intel Arc B770

Never released, and no date has been given. A gaming Celestial card is reportedly cancelled.

Go to first-party listings firstFounders Edition cards and a partner's base model are the likeliest to carry list price, though they sell out fastest.

Recent news

The stories behind the forecast.

Nov 12

GPUJensen Huang steps down as Nvidia CEO

CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO. Japanese RTX 5090 and 5080 prices then fell up to 22% and about 17%, but US prices haven't followed.

Nov 15

GPURTX 5090 prices dip in Japan

Prices reportedly fell up to 22% there after Jensen Huang stepped down as Nvidia CEO. The cuts appear limited to Japan so far.

Nov 12

GPURTX 5080 24GB reportedly scrapped

Costly 3GB GDDR7 modules are cited as the reason, so the 16GB card stays on top for now. No relief is coming from a new SKU.

Nov 11

GPUMSI hike could add up to $1,200 to the RTX 5080

A report from China also lists up to $600 more for the RTX 5070 Ti. Treat the top of that range as a worst case.

Nov 2

GPUMakers push retailers toward older cards

Supply of RTX 50 cards is described as extremely limited, so vendors are steering buyers to models like the RTX 3060.

Nov

GPUAsus and Gigabyte raise prices again

Second increase of the year across RTX 50 and Radeon RX 9000 cards, as memory costs keep climbing.

Q4

RAMPC DRAM up 13–18% this quarter

TrendForce expects PC memory supply to shrink in 2027 as capacity shifts toward servers. SSD contract prices are rising more slowly, under 5%.

Oct

RAMBernstein sees +43% in 2027, then a drop

Memory price per gigabit is forecast to rise next year and fall about 53% in 2028, though not all the way back to pre-crisis levels.

Oct

CPUIntel reportedly planning another 10% hike

Intel already lifted the Core Ultra 7 270K Plus from $299 to $349 in July. AMD's Ryzen 7 9800X3D has stayed near $445.

What this means if you're building

Buy the GPU you need nowEvery source points to higher or flat prices through mid-2027. Waiting for a dip is a gamble.
Get RAM and SSD soonBoth are forecast to keep rising next year, and relief is not expected before 2028.
CPU and PSU can waitPrices are mostly stable. The 9800X3D has barely moved, so no need to rush.
